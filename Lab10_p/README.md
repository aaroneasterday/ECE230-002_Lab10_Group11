## Lab Questions

### What is difference between edge and level sensitive circuits?
Edge sensitive circuits flip when the clock changes (falling or rising edge) and level sensitive circuits change when the clock is one. 

### Why is it important to declare initial state?
Without declaring an initial state, there is not state reported at all.

### What do edge sensitive circuits let us build?
Edge sensitive circuits let us build counters.
